SMART PANTRY MANAGER — 5–7 MINUTE VIDEO SCRIPT

0:00–1:00 — GitHub walkthrough
Show your public repository and the real commit history. Explain the progression of your work and ensure the commits were made as you developed the project. Do not fabricate commits or claim a history that does not exist.

1:00–3:30 — Live app demonstration
Open the app. Add ingredients such as egg, tomato and bread. Show them in the pantry. Edit one item and show the change. Delete an item and show that it disappears. Open Suggested Recipes and select a recipe. Remove or rename one required ingredient and refresh suggestions to prove that a recipe requiring it disappears. Add the ingredient back and show the recipe return. Close and reopen the app to show persistence.

3:30–5:45 — Explain the code
Explain (1) SQLiteOpenHelper creates the pantry and recipes tables and stores records locally; (2) the custom BaseAdapter classes populate ListView rows from database results; (3) Intents open activities and pass ingredient/recipe IDs; and (4) getStrictMatches checks every required ingredient and rejects a recipe if even one is missing. Point to the corresponding source code while speaking.

5:45–6:30 — Database choice
Explain that SQLite was chosen because the app is local-first, works offline, persists data between sessions and avoids the need for a separate backend.
